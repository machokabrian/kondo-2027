About Kondo

“A stronger, fairer Nakuru where every voice is truly heard and valued.”

Senator Kondo is running to be Nakuru County’s voice in the Senate: protecting devolution, overseeing how county funds are used, and making sure the needs of every ward reach the national table. His promise is simple: listen first, act fairly, and report back to the people.

What We Stand For

Every Voice Heard

Open forums in every ward so residents shape the decisions that affect them.

Fairness for All

Equal opportunity and fair representation for every community in Nakuru.

Jobs & Youth

Practical support for young people, small businesses and local enterprise.

Accountable Devolution

Close oversight so county money delivers real services, not waste.

Health & Education

Pushing for well-run clinics, hospitals and schools that serve families.

Farmers & Women

Backing farmers, women and persons with disabilities with fair access to resources.

What does a Senator do?

A Senator represents the county in the Senate, protects devolution, oversees county governments and helps pass laws affecting counties.
